Magnets (24 total, 12 per cup)
------------------------------
Epoxy West 105 + 206 (J03) at 22 C: pot life 20-25 min, gel 2-3 h, handling
10-15 h, full 5-7 days. Below 15 C it barely cures.
 - mix in batches of 3-4, not all 12; thicker layers cure faster
 - thicken with colloidal silica or it runs out of a vertical pocket
 - magnet sits 0.2 mm off the back face; wipe the excess before it gels
 - polarity: hold both cups face to face and drop the magnets into the second
   one - the wrong orientation pushes itself out. Then glue.
 - cover 14 closes the pockets and the clamp's four M3 x 12 pass through it,
   so retention does not depend on the glue at all

Assembly, wet side (front to back)
----------------------------------
 1. press the two iglidur bushings into the spider's Ø7 seats
 2. bolt the spider to the barrel's wet face, 4x M4 x 12
 3. rotor clamp halves around the shaft at 25..47, rotor on top, 4x M3 x 12
 4. slide shaft + rotor in from the front until the clamp butts the first
    bushing at 47
 5. wet cup (magnets in), cover 14, cup clamp halves, 4x M3 x 12 through
    the clamp and the cover into the cup; the sleeve end reaches 61
 6. thrust washer 06 into the bulkhead's recess, greased with Molykote 111
